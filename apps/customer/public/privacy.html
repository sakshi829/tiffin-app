Who can see it

  • The kitchen owner can see your information to run the service.
  • Kitchen staff see the day's packing list: your name, your tiffin and extras, and your food notes (so allergies are respected).
  • Delivery staff see only the deliveries assigned to them, from yesterday to tomorrow: your name, food notes, the place and how to get there, and what to deliver. They see your phone number only if the owner allows it.
  • Staff never see your bills, payments or messages.
  • Other customers never see your information.

These rules are enforced by the database itself, not only by the app. Each person can only ever read what they are allowed to.

How long we keep it

  • Delivery photos and voice notes: deleted automatically after 30 days (or the shorter or longer period the kitchen sets).
  • Payment screenshots: deleted automatically after about 13 months.
  • Your account details: while you are a customer, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Bills and payment records: kept as long as the law requires the kitchen to keep its accounts. After your account is deleted they no longer show your name.
  • Backups: kept encrypted for up to 90 days, then deleted.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we have about you,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it (see below),
  • and you can complain to us, and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Ask in the app's chat or at [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within 30 days.

Delete your account

  1. In the app: Me › Delete my account sends the request to the kitchen. Or email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Within 30 days we erase your name, phone, email, food notes, delivery places, messages, voice notes, photos and your phone's notification token, and sign you out.
  3. We keep your bills and payments (amounts and dates only, shown as "Deleted customer") because the kitchen must keep its accounts.

How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The owner's account needs an authenticator app (two-step sign-in), and the owner app locks with the phone's fingerprint or PIN.
  • Money records cannot be edited or deleted, and every important change is logged.
  • Nightly backups are encrypted.

Children

The service is for adults. If a child's information was given to us, ask us and we will delete it.

Terms of service (short)

  • The kitchen's current meal times, cutoffs, prices, late-skip charge and late-payment fee are shown in the app, and you agree to them before you start. Changes are shown in the app before they apply.
  • Skipping before the cutoff is free. Days when the kitchen is closed are never charged.
  • Please tell the kitchen about allergies in your food notes. Meals are home-cooked; we cannot promise a kitchen free of any allergen.
  • Pay by UPI to the kitchen's QR code or in cash. A payment counts once the kitchen confirms it.
  • Keep your sign-in email secure. If you lose your phone, use "Sign out on all my devices" or tell the kitchen.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
