# Store listing (Google Play; reuse for the App Store)

Ready-to-paste text for the **customer app**. The Marathi and Hindi versions should be read by a fluent speaker before you publish them (same as the in-app translations).

App name (max 30 characters): **Amruta's Kitchen**

## English

**Short description (max 80):**
Home-made tiffins: today's meal, where to deliver, extras, bills and UPI pay.

**Full description:**
Amruta's Kitchen brings home-made lunch and dinner tiffins to hospitals, offices and nearby workplaces.

With the app you can:
- See today's and tomorrow's meals
- Tell the kitchen where to deliver today (ward, desk, lab…)
- Skip a meal or add extras before the cutoff
- Pause meals when you go on leave
- See your bill and pay by UPI
- Chat with the kitchen, including short voice notes
- Get alerts when your meal is delivered

Your data stays with the kitchen: no GPS tracking, no contacts, no ads. Available in English, मराठी and हिन्दी.

Joining needs an invite code from the kitchen (or, if the kitchen allows it, a request it approves).
